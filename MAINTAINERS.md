## Reviewers

No formal catalog reviewers have been appointed yet. Contributors may review
any public pull request, but a record cannot gain `verified` status until its
review satisfies the independent-review process in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Emeritus maintainers

None.
